From bistro roots to haute cuisine

Le Expensziv opened when Chef Amélie Duval returned to Paris after a decade in Lyon's Michelin kitchens. Her goal was simple: serve precise French classics without losing the warmth of a neighborhood bistro.

The dining room seats forty-eight guests. On event nights we host live piano and chef's table experiences — see Hours & Location for the entertainment calendar.

Our philosophy

  • Respect tradition while embracing seasonal produce.
  • Train every server to guide guests through wine and food pairings.
  • Keep the guest experience calm, never rushed.

Rooted along the banks of Paris, blending bistro warmth with refined French cuisine. Savour seasonal delicacies paired with fine wine; join a French dinner amid piano melodies.
